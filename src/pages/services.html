Nigeria, Africa and beyond

In Nigeria I work on site: drone surveys, GNSS ground control and field checks, through to analysis and delivery.

Across Africa and further afield I work remotely. Open satellite data covers the whole continent, so most projects can start without a site visit, and where drone imagery is needed I can process what a local team collects.
