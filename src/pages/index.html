Counting oil palms from aerial imagery

For a commercial oil palm plantation, a YOLOv8 model finds each palm crown in aerial imagery and turns it into a mapped, countable tree, block by block.

{{ palm_double_counts }} double counts removed ({{ palm_double_count_share }}). Every hectare is attributed to exactly one block.

GeoAID flood susceptibility: Amuwo-Odofin pilot

A machine-learning model that maps which parts of Amuwo-Odofin are more prone to flooding, built on satellite data and published as a live dashboard.

The spatial score tests the model on areas it never saw in training, and the drop shows it is a screening tool, not a basis for decisions about specific sites.

Process

How I work

  1. Scoping call

    Twenty minutes. You tell me the decision you need to make. I tell you whether spatial data can help and what it would take.

  2. Pilot on your data

    I test the approach on a small part of your area and report its error rate, so you can judge it before you commit.

  3. Delivery you keep

    You get the results, the code that produced them and documentation your team can follow.
