Service

Flood, climate and environmental risk

Before you buy land, plan a programme or insure an asset, it helps to know where water goes and what sits in its way. I map that from satellite data and say how far the map can be trusted.

Book a 20-minute call All services

What you get

  • Flood susceptibility maps built from terrain, rainfall, land cover, soil and drainage.
  • Flood extent for past events, mapped from Sentinel-1 radar.
  • Exposure counts: people, schools, health facilities and roads in each risk tier.
  • Rainfall analysis, including near-real-time rainfall from NASA GPM IMERG.
  • Environmental change over time: vegetation loss, land conversion and urban growth.

Who it's for

Developers and investors screening sites, insurers, emergency planners, NGOs and government agencies.

How it works

  1. QuestionArea and the decision at stake

  3. ModelSusceptibility and exposure

  5. DeliverMaps, exposure tables and stated limits
