Geospatial AI and machine learning

Models that read imagery at a scale no team could manage by hand, and predictive models that tell you how far to trust them. The oil palm project counted 718,751 palms this way.

Book a 20-minute call All services

What you get

  • Object detection and counting: trees, buildings or other features in drone, aerial or satellite imagery.
  • Classification of land cover, crops or other classes you care about.
  • Predictive maps, such as flood susceptibility, built from many spatial factors.
  • Explanations of each prediction with SHAP, so you can see what drives a result.
  • An honest error rate, from tests on areas the model did not train on rather than on random samples.

Who it's for

Plantation and farm operators, lenders and insurers, planners and researchers: anyone with more imagery or more ground than people can review by hand.
