Service

Drone mapping and photogrammetry

I plan and fly drone surveys across Nigeria and process the imagery into maps you can measure from: orthomosaics, surface and terrain models, and counts of trees or other objects.

Book a 20-minute call All services

What you get

  • An orthomosaic: one georeferenced image of the whole site that you can measure on.
  • Digital surface and terrain models for heights, slopes and drainage.
  • Object counts and inventories, such as one point per tree, using the same detection approach as the oil palm project.
  • Stockpile and earthwork volumes measured from the surface model.
  • Files in standard formats (GeoTIFF, GeoPackage, point clouds where useful) and a short note on accuracy and coverage.

Who it's for

Plantation managers counting stands and finding gaps, developers and contractors tracking a site, quarry and mining teams measuring stockpiles, and anyone who needs a more detailed picture of a site than satellite imagery can give.

How it works

  1. ScopeArea, detail needed and deadline
  2. PlanFlight lines, overlap and ground control
  3. FlySurvey flights and GNSS ground control on site
  4. ProcessDJI Terra, Agisoft Metashape or WebODM
  5. DeliverMaps, models, counts and an accuracy note
