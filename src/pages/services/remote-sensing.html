Service

Satellite remote sensing

Satellite imagery covers every site on earth, again and again, and much of it is free. I turn it into maps and numbers: what is on the land, how it is changing, and where water and vegetation are.

Book a 20-minute call All services

What you get

  • Land use and land cover maps, with the accuracy of each class stated.
  • Change detection between dates: new buildings, cleared vegetation, expanded farms.
  • Vegetation and crop condition through a season, from indices such as NDVI.
  • Flood and water mapping from Sentinel-1 radar, which sees through cloud.
  • Farm and plot checks for lenders: whether a farm exists, how large it is and whether it is planted.

Who it's for

Agric lenders and insurers, NGOs monitoring programmes, government agencies, researchers, and companies that need to watch many sites without visiting them.

How it works

  2. ImagerySentinel-2 optical, Sentinel-1 radar or finer data
  3. ProcessGoogle Earth Engine or Python
