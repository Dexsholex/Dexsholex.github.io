Privacy

This site doesn't collect anything about you. The details are below, including what happens if you email me.

On this site

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, no advertising and no forms that send data.
  • If you switch between light and dark mode, or pick a language, that choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent anywhere.
  • The site doesn't look up where you are. If your browser is set to another language the site offers, it may suggest that version, and you choose.
  • The interactive map loads only files from this site. No third-party map tiles or fonts are requested.
  • The request builder on the home page writes an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To run and secure the service, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to {% if site.booking_url %}Cal.com, {% endif %}LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, {% if preprint_url %}Preprints.org {% endif %}and the GeoAID dashboard on Streamlit take you to those services, which have their own privacy terms.

I don't share them or add you to any mailing list. You can ask me at any time to delete our correspondence: {{ site.email }}.
