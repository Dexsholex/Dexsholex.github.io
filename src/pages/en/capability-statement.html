Selected work

Oil palm counting from aerial imagery

For a commercial oil palm plantation: a YOLOv8 crown-detection pipeline that mapped {{ palm.palms }} palms across {{ palm.hectares }} ha in {{ palm.blocks }} blocks. Overlapping deliveries were resolved so every hectare is counted once, removing {{ palm.double_counts }} double counts.

GeoAID flood susceptibility, Amuwo-Odofin (Lagos)

An open model with a live dashboard: structural flood susceptibility, near-real-time rainfall and exposure of people, schools, clinics and roads. ROC-AUC about 0.798 on a random split and about 0.642 under spatial cross-validation, reported side by side.

Lagos Access Map (in development)

A ward-by-ward audit of how complete OpenStreetMap's record of health facilities is in Lagos, with confidence intervals, as the base for access-to-care mapping.
