Privacy

This site collects nothing about you. Here is exactly what happens when you visit or get in touch.

On this site

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, no advertising and no forms.
  • If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent anywhere.
  • The interactive map loads only files from this site. No third-party map tiles or fonts are requested.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To run and secure the service, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to other services

Links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium and the GeoAID dashboard on Streamlit take you to those services, which have their own privacy terms.

When you contact me

If you email or message me, I use your details only to reply and to work with you. I don't share them or add you to any mailing list. You can ask me at any time to delete our correspondence: {{ email }}.
